# 3.7: Scenery format research

This subphase is research only: it changes nothing in `src/` and adds no natives.

It adds two files:
- [docs/SCENERY_FORMAT.md](../SCENERY_FORMAT.md): every opcode, the storage, coordinates and units,
  colours, the level-of-detail and visibility tests, the radio/ATIS/demo data, and crash detection.
- `tools/scenery_dump.py`: decodes the five areas from the `.ima`, and the horizon programs from
  `extracted/mem_dump.bin`.

## Results

- **The scenery is a program.** It does all culling, level of detail and hidden-line selection itself,
  using range tests on the position, `eye_pos` and the time of day. It also does the following:
  - tunes NAV/ILS/COM stations
  - sets the airport elevation, magnetic variation and UTC offset
  - holds the demo-flight key script
  - holds the ATIS texts
  - sets crash code 6 (building)
- **Units.** One integer position unit is 256 m; altitude words are metres. Three things confirm it:
  - the length of Meigs' runway
  - the distance from Meigs to Champaign
  - field elevations against the real ones
- **Local frames.** Op 24 gives each object a local frame with a shift of 4, 8 or 12 (local units of
  1/16 m, 1 m or 16 m).
- **Decoding coverage.**
  - The five areas: 35647/35647 bytes (100%). 232 of those bytes are dead code, a disabled O'Hare level of
    detail in area 0. Embedded data is 2852 bytes.
  - The horizon programs: 88/88 bytes.
  - No desynchronisation.
- **Checks.**
  - Area 0 is byte-identical to the copy at DS:3A84 in a runtime dump.
  - Sampled `scenery_ip` and `[31D1]` values from runs of 457–1500 frames land on decoded instruction
    boundaries, and every return address follows a `call`.
  - Area 0's geometry spans E 77..711, N 12..1298 (`area_bounds`: E −1000..1000, N −1000..2000). The start
    position, E 287 N 786, is Meigs: its runway block is centred exactly there.
- **Gaps in the traces.** All 33 opcodes that the programs use ran in the traces. 05, 06, 08, 0D, 0E, 10,
  11, 2A and 34 are valid but unused. 03, 0F, 42 and all unassigned opcodes hang the interpreter.
- **Crashes.**
  - 08 (SPLASH) is a pixel test: under 4 m altitude, the bottom-centre back-buffer word is the water
    pattern 2222h.
  - 02 (MOUNTAIN CRASH) is never set in 1.05.

## Interface for 3.8 (scenery interpreter in C)

**Entry points.**
- `scenery_interp` (0050:3CC0) is called from three places:
  - `draw_scenery` at 0464, normally
  - `draw_scenery` at 0499, in radar view, with altitude swapped for `radar_zoom`
  - `horizon_fill` at 2B4F, in capture mode with `eye_pos` zeroed
- None of these callers reads flags or registers after the call. So `flag_mask` = 0, and the only
  outputs are memory.
- The callers rely on DS being preserved (push/pop).

**State.** All interpreter state is in DS:
- `scenery_ip` (30C2), `[31D1]` (return address), `capture_mode`, `[30C6]`
- the line state (3163, 315D–3161, 310F–313D)
- `[31D0]`
- `eye_pos`
- the frame (30DD–30EA)
- the point cache (3288)
- the search flags (31D3/31D4/31DE/31DF)

A native must do every read and write through `ds_read*`/`ds_write*`, for two reasons:
- Ops `25` and `1A` can write any DS word.
- `--verify` compares the write logs.

**Callees.** Each needs either a native from 3.3/3.4/3.6 or a C port:
- `world_to_eye_delta`, `rotate_point`, `outcode_p1`/`p2`, `clip_project_line` (and the clip routines
  4AF7/4BCD/4CA3/4D18)
- `project_dot` (3D1A/3D26), `plot_pixel_es`, `draw_line` (including the 5060 edge-record path)
- `draw_line_list`, `build_view_matrix`, `cga_program_regs`, `clear_view_buffer`
- `atis_start` (4FBF), `poly_fill` (549B)

`project_dot` ends by jumping to `plot_pixel_es`.

**Suggested order.**
1. Port the dispatcher plus the control/data ops: 0B 17 18 19 1A 20–25 28 1D 1E. These are pure memory
   operations and easy to verify.
2. Port the colour ops.
3. Port the geometry ops, on top of the 3.6 transform natives.

The handlers are separate `call` targets, so each can be bound and `--verify`'d on its own before
`scenery_interp` itself is replaced. Each handler is entered with BX = opcode address and DS = 0618.
They clobber registers freely, but `--verify` compares registers at the handler's RET, so a per-handler
native must reproduce them. A whole-interpreter native avoids that.

**Edge cases to reproduce.**
- Opcode 42 calls through the word at DS:3288 (point-cache slot 0).
- Opcodes 03 and 0F loop forever, and the rest hang at 45B1. No program uses any of them.
- `op_origin` truncates `(pos − O) >> s` to 16 bits.
- Op 1D can match several stations in one pass; the last one wins.
- At the end, the interpreter clears 31D4/31DF and writes the capture terminator.

**Timing.** The cost per call varies a great deal with the scene, so screenshots may differ slightly when
the native charges a fixed `.cycles` (see 3.0).

## For 3.20 (scenery loading)

- An area is one boot-stream record starting at sector 0 of `area_tracks[i]`: {01, word length L,
  L−3 bytes}, read with no gaps across 4 KB tracks.
- `select_scenery_area` also clears `[0571]` and `[31E8]` and sets `[3628]` = 1.
- `read_area()` in `tools/scenery_dump.py` is a reference implementation.

## Open questions

- `[0416]` (2222/5555/FFFF): which mark `view_overlay_marks` draws with it.
- `[1A1D]`: glide slope or DME parameter? `[0915]` versus `[0917]`: two variation values, compass or VOR?
- `[1E53]` in the war area.
- What `[0406]` = 1 does exactly in `sub_1BB0`: refuel?
- The two buildings with crash boxes are 530 and 740 local metres tall. The scale is exaggerated, or the
  vertical unit isn't quite a metre.
- The edge record format at DS:3734 and the span fill (`poly_fill`, 55B7/59B7) are only summarised here.
  3.4/3.8 should document them in full.
- Areas 1–4 were checked by decoding and bounds only. The trace campaign never flies there, so no
  runtime dump of them has been compared.
